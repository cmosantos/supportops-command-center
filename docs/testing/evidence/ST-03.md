sequence, complete/incomplete outcome constraints, exact policy revision and

## Policy and boundary evidence

- Exactly 16 unique Cartesian matrix rules are validated fail-closed.
- Impact and urgency derive from closed, versioned criterion IDs; the highest
  supported criterion wins.
- A contradictory declared dimension returns `triage_incomplete`.
- `MX-HC` requires explicit critical-impact reassessment and applies `STOP-03`;
  missing reassessment yields `DQ-01` and incomplete output.
- `MX-CC` is the only P1 cell and routes to `incident_coordination` with
  `STOP-02`.
- Blocking questions always suppress priority and route and add `STOP-04`.
- Conditional and mandatory escalation reasons are additive; route precedence is
  `N1 < N2 < incident_coordination`.
- DQ-01 through DQ-12, STOP-01 through STOP-07 and the closed risk catalog are
  covered by contract/unit tests.
